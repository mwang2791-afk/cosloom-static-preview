Craft Process

From Reference to Final Fit

A six-stage commission rhythm keeps the build clear for solo clients and ambitious full-set projects alike.

01

Reference Review

Send character images, target deadline, event use, budget range, and the pieces you want built.

02

Material Planning

We map fabric, foam, resin, leather, paint, trims, and structure against comfort and finish goals.

03

Pattern & Structure

Measurements and pattern logic define the costume before sewing, shaping, or armor assembly begins.

04

Build & Sewing

Construction moves through stitch work, foam shaping, prop bases, accessories, and progress checks.

05

Fitting & Finishing

Silhouette, closure, surface paint, comfort, and camera-facing details are refined before final review.

06

Delivery

Final balance, quality check, packing plan, and worldwide shipping preparation close the commission.
