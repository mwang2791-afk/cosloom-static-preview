Studio Standards

A clear commission rhythm for solo cosplayers, creators, photographers, and small teams.

CosLoom keeps the work personal without making the process vague: references, fit, materials, updates, privacy, and shipping are treated as part of the craft.

Reference-led quote

Scope, materials, deadline, and character accuracy are reviewed before a quote is confirmed.

Made-to-measure fit

Costume work is planned around real wear, movement, comfort, and the client measurements needed for that piece.

Progress photos

Production updates help clients understand silhouette, surface finish, and important craft decisions.

Private reference review

Uploaded references and brief files are handled as private admin review material, not public gallery assets.

Worldwide shipping planning

Packing, deadlines, country, prop scale, and fragile details are considered before final delivery.

Armor & props support

CosLoom can combine textile costume craft with foam, resin, painted surfaces, crowns, accessories, and props.
