Studio Story

Cos is costume and cosplay. Loom is making, weaving, and craft.

CosLoom turns character references into wearable, camera-ready costume pieces through costume craft, material planning, and progress communication.

Why CosLoom

Many cosplay ideas sit between character art and real wear. CosLoom helps translate that space into fabric, structure, finish, and fit decisions.

What we make

Custom costumes, armor accents, helmets, props, accessories, full-set commissions, and light custom starting points for faster projects.

How we work

Reference review comes first, then quote, measurement needs, deposit rhythm, progress photos, final balance, and shipping planning.

Who we make for

Solo cosplayers, first-time custom clients, creators, photographers, performers, and small teams who need character work that feels personal.

What feels different

The process is professional without pretending to be a huge entertainment studio, and personal without becoming vague or improvised.
